Featured project

BINGA — Merchant Contract Management

Berkeley Systems, Casablanca · Software Engineering Intern · 22 June – 22 July 2026

Context

BINGA is the first cash-payment channel on the Internet in Morocco: customers pay online purchases and bills in cash at physical points of sale. Each affiliated merchant has a contract — bank details, pricing, commissions per channel — that used to live in scattered Excel files.

What I built

  • Two-step sign-in: credentials + reCAPTCHA v2, then a TOTP code; JWT access (30 min) and refresh (7 days) tokens, backup codes
  • Merchant, bank, pricing and per-channel commission management; PDF (iText) and Excel (Apache POI) exports, Excel import, KPI dashboard
  • Semantic search on bge-m3 embeddings with cosine similarity — 100 % local via Ollama
  • Conversational assistant on Qwen2.5:3B using a Strategy pattern: one intent, one executor — the model never touches the database
